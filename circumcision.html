    Overview of Circumcision

    Circumcision is a minor surgical procedure that removes the foreskin covering the tip of the penis. It is commonly performed for medical, hygienic, or personal reasons and is safe when done by experienced surgeons.

    What Is Circumcision?

    Circumcision is a simple and safe procedure in which the foreskin (the skin covering the head of the penis) is surgically removed. It may be recommended in cases of tight foreskin (phimosis), recurrent infections, pain during urination, or difficulty maintaining hygiene.

    When Is Circumcision Needed?

    • Tight foreskin that cannot be pulled back (Phimosis)
    • Pain during erection or urination
    • Repeated infections or inflammation
    • Swelling or redness of the foreskin
    • Difficulty maintaining proper hygiene

    Common Symptoms & Concerns

    • Pain or discomfort in the foreskin area
    • Infection or discharge
    • Difficulty retracting foreskin
    • Bad odor due to poor hygiene
    • Recurrent urinary infections

    Circumcision Treatment Options

    Circumcision can be performed using modern laser technology or traditional surgical methods. The right procedure depends on the patient’s condition, age, and medical recommendation.

    Laser Circumcision

    • Minimally invasive and advanced procedure
    • Less pain and minimal bleeding
    • Precise removal of foreskin
    • Faster healing and recovery
    • Same-day discharge in most cases

    Conventional Circumcision Surgery

    • Traditional surgical method using scalpel
    • May require stitches
    • Slightly longer recovery time
    • Possible post-surgery swelling
    • Recommended in certain medical conditions

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    Is circumcision a painful procedure?

    Laser circumcision is performed under anesthesia, making the procedure virtually painless. Patients may experience mild discomfort after surgery, which is manageable with prescribed medications.

    How long does circumcision surgery take?

    Laser circumcision usually takes about 20–30 minutes. In most cases, it is a same-day procedure and patients can return home shortly after.

    How long is the recovery period?

    Most patients recover within 7–10 days. Complete healing may take a few weeks, depending on individual health and proper post-surgery care.

    When is circumcision medically necessary?

    Circumcision may be recommended for conditions like phimosis (tight foreskin), recurrent infections, pain during urination, or difficulty maintaining hygiene.

    Is laser circumcision safe?

    Yes, laser circumcision is a safe and advanced procedure when performed by experienced surgeons. It offers minimal bleeding, faster recovery, and lower risk of complications.
